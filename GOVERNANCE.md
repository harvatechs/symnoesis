# Project Governance

**Status:** Proposed (interim maintainership by founding group until bodies are formally seated).

Symnoesis (SYMN-01) is an open research initiative stewarded by HarVa Groups, designed to operate transparently and not-for-profit.

## Governance Bodies

1. **Maintainers**: Responsible for day-to-day repository stewardship, pull request reviews, code quality, and release management. Requires at least 2 maintainers.
2. **Scientific Advisory Board (SAB)**: Owns constructs, statistical thresholds, weights, and the Critical-Harm Indicator (CHI) registry. Decides specification modifications via formal RFCs.
3. **Ethics Review Board**: Evaluates human-subject protocols, vulnerability protections, and participant safety nets prior to any empirical trials.
4. **Steering Council**: Provides strategic direction, ensures funding independence, monitors conflict of interest, and guides the international standards path.

## Decision Making

- **Routine Decisions**: Lazy consensus on technical issues, with a 72-hour review window.
- **Specification Revisions**: Formal RFC process (`rfcs/`) requiring review and sign-off by the Scientific Advisory Board with a 14-day community comment period.
- **Quorum & Voting**: If consensus fails, decisions require a two-thirds majority of voting members.

## Amendments and Removal

- Amendments to governance documents require approval by both the Maintainers and Steering Council.
- Removal of a member for code-of-conduct violations follows the procedure in [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
