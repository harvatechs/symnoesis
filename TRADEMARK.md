# Trademark Guidelines

"Symnoesis", "SYMN-01", "Symnoesis Map", "Symnoesis Card", and "Symnoesis Index" are trademarks of HarVa Groups.

## Allowed Nominative Use

You may use the Symnoesis marks to:
- Truthfully refer to the specification, benchmark, or software implementation.
- Describe research studies or models evaluated using the SYMN-01 methodology, provided the evidence tier (E0–E3) and version hash are clearly identified.
- Link to the official repository or project website.

## Prohibited Use

You may not:
- Use the marks in any manner that implies endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with HarVa Groups without explicit prior written authorization.
- Use "Symnoesis Certified" or claim official certification, as certification mechanisms are reserved for Phase 5 under independent audit.
- Register domain names, social media handles, or business entities containing "Symnoesis" without authorization.
