# Funding Disclosure

**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Transparency

Symnoesis is committed to 100% funding transparency. All grant awards, philanthropic donations, institutional contributions, and computing credits will be publicly documented here, including:
- Funder name and institutional affiliation
- Amount band
- Specific grant purpose and duration
- Governing restrictions

## 2. Independence Guarantee

Financial supporters and research sponsors have no authority, review rights, or veto power over:
- Specification design, scoring weights, or threshold calibration
- Benchmark evaluation results, Symnoesis Cards, or Index rankings
- Publication timing or responsible disclosure determinations

## 3. Current Funding Status

As of release v0.1.0-dev, the project has received **no external funding**. All development is conducted on an open-source volunteer basis by HarVa Groups and community contributors.
