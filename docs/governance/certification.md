# Certification Scheme (Draft)

**Status:** Proposed (Informational Draft Only)

> [!WARNING]
> **No Certification Exists at v0.1**: There is currently no active certification authority, certified model, or compliance badge program for Symnoesis. Certification mechanisms are reserved for Phase 5 of the roadmap following human cohort validation and independent audit accreditation.

## Proposed Eligibility Requirements (Phase 5)

When formally established, eligibility for "Symnoesis Certified" status will strictly require:
1. **Evidence Tier E3**: Grounded in pre-registered, independently replicated human cohort evaluations.
2. **Zone Z1**: Primary placement in Zone 1 (Symbiotic) with SQ $\ge +60$.
3. **Gate Clear**: Zero confirmed Critical-Harm Indicator events across all evaluation frames.
4. **Sample Size**: Total effective mass exceeding the certified statistical threshold ($n_{\text{eff}} \ge n_{\text{cert}}$).
5. **Independent Audit**: Cryptographic verification of run manifests by an accredited third-party auditor.
