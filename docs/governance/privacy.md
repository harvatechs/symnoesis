# Privacy Policy

**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Website and Portal Privacy

- **Zero Tracking**: The Symnoesis public website and Interactive Map Portal use zero third-party analytics, zero advertising trackers, zero tracking cookies, and zero external tracking pixels.
- **Client-Side Edge Execution**: All calculations within the Interactive Map Portal execute 100% locally in your web browser. No responses, scores, or identifiers are transmitted to any server.
- **Local Storage**: Results and form inputs are ephemeral by default. Saving answers locally requires an explicit opt-in using local browser storage (`localStorage`), which can be cleared at any time.

## 2. Regulatory Alignment

The privacy architecture is engineered to comply by construction with:
- European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU 2016/679)
- India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act)
- California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA
