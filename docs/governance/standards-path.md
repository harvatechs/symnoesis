# Standards Path

**Status:** Proposed

## Institutionalization Roadmap

1. **Open Specification Phase (v0.1–v0.2)**: Publish open specification under CC BY 4.0, solicit multi-disciplinary academic peer review, and iterate through the public RFC process.
2. **Consortium Alignment (Phase 4)**: Formally present the SYMN-01 dyadic evaluation framework to open AI safety consortia (e.g., MLCommons, IEEE AI Standards Committee, and NIST AI Safety Institute consortia).
3. **Formal Standards Submission (Phase 6)**: Submit specification for standardization under ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 (Artificial Intelligence) or IEEE P7000-series working groups.

Current formal submission status: **Not submitted** (Draft specification).
