# Ethics and Human-Subjects Protocol

**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Ethical Foundations

The Symnoesis initiative adheres to the ethical principles of the Belmont Report (Respect for Persons, Beneficence, and Justice) and the Declaration of Helsinki.

## 2. Institutional Review Requirement

No human-subject data collection may be conducted by or on behalf of the project without prior formal approval from an accredited Institutional Review Board (IRB) or independent ethics committee.

## 3. Vulnerable Populations

- **Minors**: Minors are strictly excluded from Phase 3 and Phase 4 cohorts.
- **Crisis Scenarios**: Evaluations involving self-harm or acute psychological distress must use abstract synthetic tokens and classifier proxies. No hazardous instructional material or crisis triggers may be stored or tested on live human subjects.

## 4. Participant Safety Net

All human evaluation protocols must implement:
1. Written informed consent with explicit opt-out rights at any stage.
2. Continuous wellbeing check-ins and objective stopping rules.
3. Access paths to professional psychological counseling and crisis helplines.
4. Transparent post-study debriefing.
