# Conflict of Interest Policy

**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Transparency Mandate

All Maintainers, Scientific Advisory Board members, and Steering Council participants must file an annual Conflict of Interest (COI) disclosure detailing commercial relationships, funding sources, equity holdings, and consulting roles with AI model developers.

## 2. Recusal Rules

- Any member with an active financial, employment, or governance interest in an evaluated AI vendor must recuse themselves from scoring reviews, audit determinations, or certification votes involving that vendor.
- Evaluated labs may submit their own telemetry or systems for benchmarking, but have zero influence over benchmark scoring or rank assignment.

## 3. Auditor Independence

Independent auditors appointed to verify Index editions must rotate periodically and must have no commercial consulting engagements with the systems evaluated during their audit cycle.
