# Project Charter

**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Mission

Symnoesis is an open research initiative established to develop, maintain, and publish the SYMN-01 specification, benchmark, and open-source tooling for evaluating long-term human-AI interaction.

## 2. Core Principles

1. **Dyad-First**: The unit of evaluation is the human and the AI system coupled over time.
2. **Longitudinal**: Harms and benefits are evaluated across extended multi-session windows.
3. **Safety Primacy**: Safety gates enforce hard ceilings that averages cannot hide.
4. **Honest Uncertainty**: Every score carries an explicit evidence tier and confidence interval.
5. **Privacy by Construction**: No raw conversation text retention; only derived features are processed.

## 3. Dissolution Clause

In the event that the Symnoesis project dissolves or ceases active stewardship, all specifications, documentation, software, datasets, and intellectual property shall permanently remain public domain or openly licensed under their respective Apache-2.0 and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licenses.
