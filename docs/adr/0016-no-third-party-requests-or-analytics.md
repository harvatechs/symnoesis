# 0016. No analytics or third-party requests, ever, without Ethics Review Board RFC

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: Accepted

## Context and Problem Statement
Normative requirement from spec/SPEC.md and architecture design.

## Decision Outcome
Self-host fonts, eliminate third-party scripts, and forbid tracking cookies on all public web properties.

## Consequences
- Positive: Clear normative grounding and exact alignment with specification.
- Negative: Requires formal RFC process to modify in future versions.
