# 0011. Keep 4 + 4 variables with adaptive resolution

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: Proposed

## Context and Problem Statement
Normative requirement from spec/SPEC.md and architecture design.

## Decision Outcome
Maintain full 8-variable construct space (256 pixels) with adaptive coarsening to 8x8 or 4x4 for sparse data.

## Consequences
- Positive: Clear normative grounding and exact alignment with specification.
- Negative: Requires formal RFC process to modify in future versions.
