# 0019. Portal language is descriptive and non-diagnostic

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: Accepted

## Context and Problem Statement
Normative requirement from spec/SPEC.md and architecture design.

## Decision Outcome
Enforce strict non-diagnostic, conditional phrasing on portal outputs; forbid clinical assertions about visitors.

## Consequences
- Positive: Clear normative grounding and exact alignment with specification.
- Negative: Requires formal RFC process to modify in future versions.
